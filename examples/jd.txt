Senior Backend Engineer — Platform Team

We are hiring a Senior Backend Engineer to design and operate the services that
power our payments platform. You will own backend APIs end to end, make
system-design decisions for high-throughput services, and mentor other
engineers.

Responsibilities:
- Design and build reliable, well-tested Python services and REST APIs.
- Make architecture decisions for systems handling thousands of requests/sec.
- Ensure correctness, observability, and resilience (retries, idempotency).
- Collaborate closely with product and communicate trade-offs clearly.

Requirements:
- Strong Python and API design experience.
- Solid grasp of distributed systems, databases, caching, and scaling.
- A track record of debugging production issues under pressure.
- Clear written and verbal communication.

Nice to have:
- Experience with payments/financial systems.
- Familiarity with AWS, Postgres, and event-driven architectures.
